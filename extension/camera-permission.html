One quick choice before you start: allow your laptop camera for QR scanning, or skip it and use pasted links and uploaded images.

Choose Enable camera, then Allow in Chrome’s prompt.

If Chrome already blocked Linklight, open camera settings and remove its blocked entry or change it to Allow. Then return here and try again.

On Mac: System Settings → Privacy & Security → Camera → enable Google Chrome. A managed browser may require your administrator’s help.

This checks access only. The camera turns off immediately. No microphone, recording or upload.
